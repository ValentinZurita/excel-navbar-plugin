# AppSource submission materials

## Goal

Prepare the listing and certification materials needed to submit Sheet Navigator as an Office Add-in in Microsoft Partner Center.

## Tasks

- [x] Draft store listing copy and keywords
  - Evidence: `docs/appsource/listing.md` contains proposed title, summary, HTML-ready description, feature bullets, target audience, keywords, category/industry suggestions, pricing/availability disclosure, exact legal/support URLs, and Partner Center fields requiring human decisions.
- [x] Draft reviewer certification instructions
  - Evidence: `docs/appsource/certification-notes.md` contains Excel on the web, Windows, and Mac instructions; manifest facts; no-account/no-purchase/no-SSO disclosures; feature tests; shortcuts; expected behavior; limitations; accessibility notes; and privacy/data handling notes.
- [x] Record Partner Center metadata, legal URLs, and manifest facts
  - Evidence: `docs/appsource/listing.md`, `docs/appsource/certification-notes.md`, and `docs/appsource/submission-checklist.md` record the production manifest URL, privacy URL, terms/EULA URL, support URL, repository URL, license facts, and human-owned Partner Center decisions.
- [x] Prepare screenshot candidates and identify capture gaps
  - Evidence: `docs/appsource/screenshot-plan.md` inventories existing GIF/icon assets and records the remaining need for at least one final static screenshot captured from a real Excel client.
- [x] Verify the submission checklist and open decisions
  - Evidence: `docs/appsource/submission-checklist.md` groups readiness and remaining work by Partner Center setup, package/legal links, listing assets, certification notes, validation, and final submission.

## Remaining gaps

- Partner Center enrollment, offer creation, live field entry, manifest validation, and Review and publish submission require a human publisher action.
- Final category/industry, market availability, pricing/commercial model, commercial permission process, and release timing require human account/business decisions.
- At least one final static screenshot must be captured from a real Excel client; no fake screenshot files were created.
- Current Partner Center screenshot/icon dimensions, counts, accepted formats, and localization requirements must be confirmed in the live submission UI before upload.
- Production manifest and HTTPS legal/support URLs must be validated by Partner Center at submission time.
