Privacy

Privacy Policy

Sheet Navigator is designed as a local-first Excel add-in. It does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data, credentials, or identifying information.

Last updated: May 26, 2026

No collection of personal information

Sheet Navigator does not run external servers, databases, analytics, telemetry, or tracking scripts that collect or store your information.

Workbook and spreadsheet data

To provide worksheet listing, sheet previews, groups, and drag-and-drop reordering, the add-in interacts with your active Microsoft Excel workbook through the official Microsoft Office JavaScript API.

  • It reads sheet names and visibility status from the active workbook so they can be displayed in the sidebar.
  • Groups and layout settings are saved locally using workbook custom properties or local storage.
  • Worksheet previews render a bounded image locally from workbook range data so you can identify sheets before switching.
  • No sheet content, cell data, preview image, or workbook structure is transmitted by the add-in.

Static hosting and fonts

The add-in is hosted statically on GitHub Pages. When Excel loads the taskpane, it fetches static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and asset files from GitHub's servers. GitHub may collect standard server logs, such as IP address and user-agent, for security and operational purposes under the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not have access to or control over those logs.

The public landing page loads the Inter font family from Google Fonts. When your browser requests those font resources, Google may receive standard request metadata such as your IP address, browser user-agent, referring page, and related connection details under Google's own policies. The add-in itself does not send workbook data to Google.
